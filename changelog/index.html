What's new

The same notes the app shows after an update. Every file of every release is on GitHub.

3.0.0Latest

October 25, 2026 · Downloads

  • A DAW plugin: put DAW Project Manager on your DAW's master bus to see and tick off the project's to-dos, edit its notes, and keep each playback pass as the project's latest capture, ready to play here.
  • Work sessions can follow your DAW: one ends when the DAW closes the project, and one can start when it opens (Settings > DAW plugin).
  • Play a MIDI keyboard into the piano roll, and record takes over the loop region with a metronome and latency calibration.
  • Google Drive sync now merges every device's changes instead of overwriting them, shows what it's doing, carries deletions, and can recover an earlier backup.
  • Deleted projects wait 30 days in Recently deleted, and a song can move to another profile with all its versions, notes and to-dos.
  • Moved a project folder yourself? Relocate it in Settings > Project Folders, and its projects, exclusions and recently deleted songs follow.
  • Experimental: build a sample pack from the samples your projects use, sorted by kind, with a report of how many songs use each one.
  • Experimental: export a melodic MIDI catalog, your Cubase projects' track layout, and a page of every insert chain you use in Cubase.
  • On Windows, a new installer keeps the app up to date by itself; copies from the previous installer keep updating as before.
  • Launch at login now works on the Microsoft Store build, macOS, Linux and Flatpak, and repairs itself after an update.
  • Searching notes and the changelog matches words where they start, without fuzzy surprises.
  • The macOS app now runs on Apple silicon only; Intel Macs can keep using version 2.9.
  • Settings > DAW plugin no longer says the plugin's captures become the preview song: each one is the project's latest capture.

2.9.3

October 8, 2026 · Downloads

  • Edit MIDI clips in the piano roll: draw, select, move, resize and delete notes with select, range, pencil and eraser tools, then save the result as a new clip in a collection.
  • The piano roll has its own tempo, time signature and loop region, highlights the project's scale, plays the notes you click, and lists every shortcut under ? or F1.
  • Start a new clip from scratch in any collection.
  • Collections get folders, clip roles and file-name templates: drag clips and folders around, and rename many clips to your naming scheme at once.
  • Table column widths are remembered, and every column, including your own fields, can be switched on separately in the projects table and the release tracklist.
  • On Windows, if you installed with the .exe installer, the update prompt now offers the new installer instead of the Microsoft Store.

2.9.2

October 3, 2026 · Downloads

  • See what's inside a project — track counts, the plug-ins it loads and its MIDI clips — for Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, REAPER and FL Studio (tracks for Studio One).
  • Play a project's MIDI clips with a built-in synth: 16 instruments picked from the track name, at the project tempo or your own, on a seamless loop if you like.
  • Open any clip in a zoomable piano roll with a playback line, a lane for velocity, pitch bend and controllers, and a ruler you can click to jump.
  • A new MIDI tab gathers every unique clip across your projects, arrangeable by tempo, with collections of your favourites that sync and back up.
  • Save, share or drag clips into your DAW as .mid files carrying the project's tempo and key, or import .mid files from anywhere into a collection.
  • Add your own project fields, show them as columns, and choose which columns the projects table shows.

2.9.0

September 27, 2026 · Downloads

  • Version stacks keep every version of a song under one main project with shared notes, todos, deadline and work time — stack by hand, or automatically by folder with the new Version Stack scan mode.
  • Build your own colour themes and share them as a .dpmtheme file.
  • Give a project its own cover art, colour and icon — projects you don't decorate look exactly as before.
  • A new card view shows your library as a gallery of covers, next to the table.
  • Todos can have their own due dates: the Tasks tab puts what's due next first and marks overdue tasks, and Android reminds you ahead of time.
  • Attach files and links to a project — a reference track, lyric sheet or stems link — and export them together.
  • Move a project to another folder from inside the app; its paths and attachments follow.
  • Archive a finished project into a checked zip outside your library, and restore it whenever you need it.
  • Tag projects with your own labels and filter, search and sort by them — turn tags on in Settings › Appearance.
  • Ctrl/Cmd-click and Shift-click select several rows in Projects, Templates and Releases, and releases can be deleted in bulk.
  • A "What's New" summary appears once after each update, and Settings now has a full changelog going back to 1.0.
  • Release tracks show each song's length, parts progress (click it to manage parts) and your notes, with the release's total running time at the bottom.
  • A song's length is read from its preview song automatically, or can be typed on the project page.
  • A release's tracks can also be viewed as a sortable table.
  • Creating a release offers to reuse a selected track's cover art as the release artwork.
  • Cover art shows in the mobile player, the mini player and the lock-screen notification.
  • Only one song on a release plays at a time, and audio files show their length before you press play.
  • Player shortcuts: Ctrl+scroll (⌘+scroll on macOS) changes the volume, M toggles mono, and the volume is remembered.
  • Project folders can be switched off instead of removed — nothing is deleted — and the project counts now match the list.
  • Tasks tab: tasks are ticked off with the checkbox only, and a ticked task stays visible with an Undo button until you leave the tab.
  • Breadcrumbs in the title bar show where you are and take you back in one click.
  • Cover art and accent colours fade in from the right of the projects list, and card covers fade at the bottom so their buttons stay visible.

2.8.0

September 6, 2026 · Downloads

  • Parts & performers — track a song part by part (instrument, performer, take status), in a full workspace page with filters, bulk actions and reorder, plus reusable part templates and CSV/Excel export & re-import.
  • REAPER markers and regions — extracted from the project file and turned into a clickable table of contents; tapping one seeks the preview player.
  • Logic Pro & LUNA finally behave — launching, "Open folder" and pending-folder resolution all work for package-bundle projects, and Logic projects now yield BPM, key and app version.
  • DAW Locations on every desktop platform — Windows and macOS get the executable override that was Linux-only, and a DAW can now hold several locations (e.g. two Ableton versions) with a picker at launch.
  • Redesigned preview waveform — a peak/RMS bar rendering with real MP3 decoding instead of a header estimate, optional stereo lanes, and a Classic style kept as an option.
  • Search that narrows as you type — substring matching first, with a far stricter fuzzy fallback, and DAW project notes are now searchable.
  • Sectioned project detail layout — an optional left-rail layout for the long detail page, matching Settings.

2.7.4

August 12, 2026 · Downloads

  • Hide DAW-generated date stamps in project names (new setting).
  • "Find automatically" when a preview song is missing — clears the dead reference and re-runs mixdown detection. Desktop only, where detection actually runs.
  • iOS now matches Android for preview-song sharing and background audio: audio is transcoded to AAC/.m4a via AVAssetExportSession, and playback continues with lock-screen controls when the app loses focus.
  • Mobile long-press opens an action sheet (Play / Share / Details / Select) instead of jumping straight into multi-select. Once selecting, long-press is a plain toggle again.
  • BPM and key are edited on the detail page, not inline in the grid. Inline editing put a bordered text field inside the cell — the source of the stray "selected" highlight — and drag-selection no longer paints a range.
  • The title bar version string drops its leading v.
  • Android download is roughly half the size: a debug arm64 APK goes from 228.5 MB to 112.7 MB.
  • Android transcoding uses the platform's own MediaCodec via a ~200-line Kotlin converter instead of a bundled ffmpeg plugin.
