What's new

The biggest release yet. Here is what it brings — and every change, version by version.

Experimental · Sample pack

Every sample your songs use, in one pack

The kicks, loops and one-shots your projects play — from audio clips and from samplers — copied into one folder, sorted by kind, with a report of how many songs use each one.

  • Each sample once, however many projects copied it.
  • Samples that moved are found again in your sample library folders.
  • Your own recordings, bounces and stems stay out unless you ask, and mastering sessions are skipped.
  • Your MIDI collections can go in the pack too.
  • Export again later: only what's new is added. Optionally renamed with their BPM and key.

New · DAW plug-in

Your to-dos, inside your DAW

A plug-in for the master bus of any DAW that loads VST3 — or AU on macOS. Audio passes through untouched.

  • Pre-listen capture: each playback pass becomes the project's latest capture in the app, ready to play — no bouncing.
  • To-dos and notes in the DAW: tick them off, add new ones, stamp one with the playhead time.
  • Work sessions follow the DAW: the app knows when the project opens and closes.
  • Knows which project is open, from the DAW's window or the app's launch.
Get the plug-in
New · MIDI keyboard

Play and record into the piano roll

Plug in a MIDI keyboard and the piano roll plays it: hear each key, and see it lit on the keyboard.

  • Record takes over the loop region, cycling as many passes as you like — a whole take is one undo.
  • A metronome on every beat, in time with the clip.
  • Latency calibration: tap along to a click and recordings land where you played them.

Sync that merges

Project Management

Manage your DAW projects from a single application. No more folders. Never lose track of a project.

Inside every project

Advanced Metadata Extraction

DAW version, BPM, musical key and project notes — read from every project, no manual tagging. For Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, FL Studio and Reaper it goes further: tracks of each kind, the plug-ins it loads, and every MIDI clip.

Preview

Finds your mixdowns

Export a mix from your DAW and the app finds the file on its own, ready to play.

Tasks

TODO lists

A checklist per project, and every unfinished task across your library in one queue.

MIDI

Every MIDI clip, without opening the DAW

The riffs, chords and drum patterns in every project. Play any clip through the built-in synth, edit it in the piano roll, and drag it into your DAW as a .mid — tempo and key included.

Work sessions

How long has this song taken?

In Session Mode you start a session on a project, and the time you work on it adds up: per session, and in total.

Versions
